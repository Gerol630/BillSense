How BillSense Works

BillSense looks at your bill amount, due date, cash on hand, credit card grace period, interest rates, minimum payments, and paycheck timing. Then it compares:

  • Paying with cash today
  • Using your credit card within the grace period
  • Using your credit card with interest
  • Making a minimum payment now
  • Waiting until your paycheck
  • Paying late and taking the fee

For each bill, BillSense chooses the option with the lowest real cost — including fees, interest, and cash‑flow impact. You see your recommended method, date, total cost, savings vs doing nothing, risk score, and whether waiting is safer.
